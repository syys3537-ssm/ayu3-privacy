Data We Collect

AYU3 does not collect any data.

There is no account registration. Your recordings, thumbnails, and settings are stored only on your device. The developer operates no servers and never receives your data. No analytics tools, no advertising SDKs, no tracking.

In-App Purchase (AYU3 Plus)

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase called “AYU3 Plus”. All purchase and restore processing is handled by Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). The developer never receives your name, payment details, or any other personal information.

Information related to purchases is handled according to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Device Features We Use

CameraTo record 3-second videos
MicrophoneTo record audio with your videos (if denied, videos are recorded without sound)
Photo Library (add only)To export videos when you choose to

These are used only for recording and exporting, and nothing is ever sent off your device.

Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete any recording at any time from within the app. Deleting the app removes all recordings from your device.
