Data We Collect

AYU3 does not collect any data.

There is no account registration. Your recordings, thumbnails, and settings are stored on your device. Locations (only if you turn on “Location” below) are stored on your device as well. The developer operates no servers and never receives your data. No analytics tools, no advertising SDKs, no tracking.

iCloud backup (optional)

Only if you turn on “Back up to iCloud” under Settings › Backup, your recordings (the original videos) together with their capture time, finish settings, and location (if recorded) are stored in the iCloud of your own Apple Account (a CloudKit private database). It is your own iCloud and uses your iCloud storage. The developer cannot view or receive its contents.

It is off by default. Turning it off stops further uploads (data already stored can be deleted from Settings › Apple Account › iCloud on your iPhone). Deleting a recording in the app also deletes the same recording from iCloud; there is no way to bring it back from iCloud. After changing phones or reinstalling, use “Restore from iCloud” in the app to bring your recordings back.

Location (optional)

Only if you turn on “Record where you filmed” in Settings, AYU3 saves where each clip was taken (latitude, longitude, altitude, and their accuracy). It is off by default. AYU3 asks for location access on the screen that introduces this feature, or when you turn it on in Settings (“While Using the App” only). Your location is used only while the camera is open.

Locations are stored only on your device, and in your own iCloud if iCloud backup is on. The developer never receives them.

Looking up place names: so that you can find clips with place words such as “Kyoto” or “zoo”, AYU3 sends only a latitude and longitude rounded to about 100 m to Apple’s maps service (MapKit) and receives place names. No videos, capture dates, or precise locations are sent, and each place is sent only once. The recipient is Apple, which handles it under Apple’s Privacy Policy; the developer receives nothing. The place names are stored only on your device, and are removed the next time you open the app after you delete every clip taken at that place. If you turn off “Search by Place Name” in Settings, AYU3 does not send anything to look up place names (you can still record locations).

Videos you “Save to Photos” carry the location as a Photos location (when sharing from Photos, you can turn Location off under Options). Videos you share directly from AYU3 carry no location.

Turning off “Record where you filmed” stops recording new locations and stops looking up place names. A clip’s location is deleted when you delete that clip.

In-App Purchase (AYU3 Plus)

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase called “AYU3 Plus”. All purchase and restore processing is handled by Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). The developer never receives your name, payment details, or any other personal information.

Information related to purchases is handled according to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Device Features We Use

CameraTo record 3-second videos
MicrophoneTo record audio with your videos (if denied, videos are recorded without sound)
Photo Library (add only)To export videos when you choose to
Location (While Using, optional)To save where each clip was taken, when “Record where you filmed” is on

These are used only for recording, keeping your records, and exporting. Except for the optional iCloud backup described above, nothing is ever sent off your device.

Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete any recording at any time from within the app (with iCloud backup on, the copy in iCloud is deleted as well). Deleting the app removes all recordings from your device; a backup stored in iCloud remains until you delete it from your iPhone’s Settings.
